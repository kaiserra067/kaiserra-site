What we build

  • Ra Strategy

    Starting development

    A real-time strategy game set in World War II, starting with Germany and the Soviet Union. You pick a nation, begin with one district officer, and raise a command camp. From there you build iron, food and ammunition production, and your population decides how much food and ammunition you burn through. Single-player scenarios and multiplayer on a chosen map are both planned.

  • Minecraft mods - servers

    Old product, completed

    Gameplay changes for Minecraft, from simple number tweaks to new interfaces and new mechanics, along with server work. Built and tested with AI.

  • Grand strategy mods

    Old product, completed

    Mods for games in the Hearts of Iron IV mold, where supply, production and combat all come down to formulas that have to balance. We built and balanced them with AI.

How we work

Rules first. Every system starts as a plain description of how it should behave: how fast a camp produces iron, how much food a unit eats, what a mod should change.

AI writes the code. We hand the description to AI, run what comes back, and iterate until it behaves.

Math gets checked. Strategy games live or die on their numbers. We test the formulas on their own before they go anywhere near a player.

Small and fast. A small team with AI can try more ideas in a week than a large one can in a month, and drop the ones that aren't fun.
